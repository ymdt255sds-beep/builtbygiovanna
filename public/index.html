A few useful details

You bring the content. You supply the text and photos. I add final touches: light proofreading, formatting, and small wording adjustments. Writing from scratch and creating photos are outside the included scope.

Two rounds of feedback. Each revision round is one consolidated feedback list on the agreed design and content. New pages or features need a separate quote.

30 days of fixes. For 30 days from launch, I’ll fix issues with the delivered site. New features and content updates fall outside this included fix period.

A quote built around your needs. Starting prices are a guide; your fixed quote depends on the agreed scope. Booking systems, calendar integrations, online payments, and ongoing updates are quoted separately. Domain, hosting, and third-party subscription costs are separate from the website build.
